# LGCOMIN art direction

Use `docs/phase-0-research.md` as the visual baseline and `logoBlancoLG.png` as the only official mark.

## Character

Aim for technical precision, editorial confidence and controlled motion. The work should feel dark, premium and current, with enough warmth and clarity for clients and recruiters. It is a creative developer portfolio, not a cyberpunk demo or SaaS landing page.

## Logo

- Preserve the supplied PNG, alpha, proportions and internal artwork.
- Derive composition from its circle, radial cuts and alignment.
- Give it a single meaningful hero moment, then use it sparingly.
- Do not redraw, recolor into a new brand, stretch, crop tightly or tile it as a pattern.

## Typography and color

- Use a contemporary display sans for hierarchy and a mono face only for technical metadata.
- Keep body copy highly readable with restrained line length and line height.
- Base: charcoal/near-black. Foreground: warm white. Accent: acid green. Secondary accent: cool cyan.
- Accents indicate focus, action or depth; they are not default text colors.

## Composition

- Use controlled max-widths, asymmetric editorial layouts, large project surfaces and precise separators.
- Alternate density and whitespace to create rhythm without empty screens.
- Prefer one strong idea per section. Avoid grids of identical cards when hierarchy can tell the story.
- Mobile layouts are recomposed, not shrunken.

## Rejection criteria

Reject changes that introduce generic gradient blobs, excessive glow/blur, glass cards everywhere, fake metrics, stock developer imagery, repeated logo wallpaper, random color additions, oversized pill buttons or decorative effects that compete with the content.
