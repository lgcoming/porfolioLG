# LGCOMIN content integrity

The public site snapshot recorded in `docs/phase-0-research.md` is the source of truth for initial content.

- Do not invent employment, experience, clients, projects, case studies, outcomes, testimonials, awards, metrics or technologies.
- Keep the real projects, descriptions, stacks and URLs unless Luis explicitly changes them.
- Keep the actual services and mark GEO as “Próximamente” while that qualifier remains in the source.
- Keep only real social/contact links.
- Small spelling, accent and punctuation corrections are allowed when meaning does not change; record material copy changes explicitly.
- Do not turn cautious claims into guarantees. Preserve the 48-hour response wording unless instructed otherwise.
- Metadata and JSON-LD must reflect only facts already present in the repository or explicitly provided by Luis.
- If a design needs more content, change composition rather than fabricate filler.

Before merging copy work, compare the affected data against `docs/phase-0-research.md` and call out any intentional semantic change.
